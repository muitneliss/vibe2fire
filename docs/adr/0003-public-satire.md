# 0003. The site is public and names the Omninoise studio

- **Status:** Accepted; the "public" part is superseded by 0007
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Deciders:** Cuong Tran (owner)

## Context

omninoise-ops ADR 0004 keeps the Omninoise name out of public posts and bios. The Vibe2Fire comic is a satire about how that studio was built, names it, and links its internal write-up. Asked whether to protect the site, scrub the name, or publish as-is, the owner chose to publish as-is on 2026-10-30.

## Decision

vibe2fire.dev is public and unchanged. omninoise-ops records the matching exception in its own ADR. The page keeps its notice that the marketing team is fictional and that the art is AI-generated.

## Consequences

- Anyone can read the comic and the feed.
- Social posts Omninoise makes from the feed still follow Omninoise's rules: no studio name, `lowbit.link` or internal URLs in captions, sources for facts.
- 2026-10-31: the owner dropped the word "satire" (châm biếm) from the site and the comic; the copy now says the marketing team went because it would not learn AI. The notice that the marketing team's characters are fictional stays.
- 2026-10-31: the comic is labelled "truyện hư cấu" (a work of fiction) instead of "truyện nội bộ" (an internal comic); the note keeps saying the marketing team is fictional. The write-up's technical log stays labelled as real.
